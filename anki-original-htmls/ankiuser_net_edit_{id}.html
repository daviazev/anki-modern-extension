🌟 what does vessels mean in The harbor is full of vessels, from small fishing boats to large cargo ships.?

contextual explanation:

In this sentence, vessels is a word used to describe all the different types of ships and boats (fishing boats, cargo ships) floating in the harbor.

definition:

A ship or large boat.

examples:

  • The naval vessel approached the coast. (A embarcação naval aproximou-se da costa.)
  • A fishing vessel was reported missing. (Uma embarcação de pesca foi dada como desaparecida.)

synonyms and antonyms:

Synonyms: ship, boat, craft. Antonyms: (none).

usage warning:

⚠️ 'Vessel' has other important meanings: 1. A container (like a bowl or cup). 2. A tube in the body (like a 'blood vessel'). Context is essential.

tips:

📝 'Vessel' is often a slightly more formal or technical term for a boat or ship.

✅ summary: vessel = a large boat or ship.
